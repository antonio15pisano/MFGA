League Info & Rules

Official format

The Rules

  1. League fee is due by draft day; failure to pay costs a 1st-round pick.
  2. One keeper is due one week before draft day; failure to submit means no keeper.
  3. Keeper costs the round in which they were drafted the prior year and must remain on the team all year.
  4. Trophy is handed out at draft day and returned 2–3 weeks before the next draft for engraving; damage is paid by whoever causes it.
  5. All lineup spots must be filled before the last game of the week. A roster slot with under a 0.1 projection scores −10 for that slot.
  6. Trading will be decided by a committee of Antonio, the previous season winner and the current 12 seed. If any party involved in the trade is a committee member, we will work up from the bottom of the standings to the next eligible team.
  7. After the regular season, eliminated teams may not add/drop players; doing so costs a keeper.
  8. During the draft, every drafted player must be written on the draft sheet or they will not be added.
  9. Tiebreaker: H2H, then points scored. For 3+ tied teams, all teams are pooled; division tiebreakers are settled before wildcard, then loser bracket.
  10. Direct sabotage can result in loss of a keeper or banishment.
  11. Let's Make Football Great Again.

Draft Pick Format

  • Picks 1–6 are a draft lottery: worst record gets 1 vote, best non-playoff team gets 6.
  • Picks 7–8 go to Round 1 playoff losers; higher seed gets pick 8.
  • Picks 9–10 go to Round 2 playoff losers; higher seed gets pick 10.
  • Pick 12 goes to the champion; pick 11 goes to the runner-up.

Schedule Format

  • After keepers, last year's division leaders draft their divisions snake-style, best team first.
  • Four divisions, three teams each.
  • Week 1 is Rivalry Week.
  • Weeks 3, 8 and 13 are Divisional Champions Weeks, subject to NFL bye weeks.
  • Weeks 1–14 regular season; Weeks 15–17 playoffs.

Scoring System

Passing

  • Every 10 passing yards = 0.5
  • TD pass = 4
  • Interception thrown = −2
  • 2pt conversion = 2
  • 300–399 pass yards = 2.5
  • 400+ pass yards = 5
  • Interception returned for TD = −2

Receiving

  • Per receiving yard = 0.1
  • Reception = 1
  • TD reception = 6
  • 2pt receiving conversion = 2
  • 100–199 receiving yards = 2.5
  • 200+ receiving yards = 5

Rushing

  • Per rushing yard = 0.1
  • TD rush = 6
  • 2pt rushing conversion = 2
  • 100–199 rushing yards = 2.5
  • 200+ rushing yards = 5

Kicking

  • PAT made = 1
  • PAT missed = −1
  • FG missed = −1
  • 30–39 yd FG = 3
  • 40–49 yd FG = 4
  • 50–59 yd FG = 5
  • 60+ yd FG = 6

Miscellaneous

  • Kickoff return TD = 6
  • Punt return TD = 6
  • Fumble recovered for TD = 6
  • Fumble lost = −2
  • Interception return TD = 6
  • Fumble return TD = 6
  • Blocked punt/FG return TD = 6
  • 2pt return = 2
  • Safety = 1
