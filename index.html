2026 Format

Regular Season Schedule

Weeks 1–14 are regular season. Week 1 is Rivalry Week; Weeks 3, 8 and 13 are Divisional Champions Weeks, subject to NFL bye-week changes.

Schedule Structure

Each team plays division members twice, rivals twice, and everyone else once. Weeks 15–17 are one-week playoff matchups.

Schedule Codes

Constitution

Rules, Money & Format

League Fee

League fee money is due by draft day; failure to pay results in loss of a 1st-round draft pick.

Keeper

1 keeper due 1 week before draft day. The keeper uses the draft round in which the player was selected and must remain on the team for the whole year.

Trophy

Trophy is handed out at draft day and returned roughly 2–3 weeks before draft day for engraving. Damage is paid by whoever causes it.

Lineups

All lineup spots must be filled prior to the last game of the week. A roster slot with under a 0.1 projection results in -10 points in that slot.

Trades

Trading is held by league vote. Majority rules; a tie is declined.

After Elimination

After the regular season ends, a non-playoff team may no longer add/drop players. Doing so results in loss of a keeper.

Draft Sheets

During the draft, all teams receive a Draft Sheet. Every drafted player must be written down or they will not be added to the team.

Tiebreakers

Tiebreaker procedure is H2H, then points scored. If 3+ teams are tied, all teams are pooled. Division tiebreakers are settled first, followed by wildcard, then loser bracket.

Sabotage

Direct sabotage can result in loss of keeper or banishment depending on the incident.

Scoring

Scoring System

Passing

Every 10 passing yards = 0.5; passing TD = 4; interception thrown = -2; 2-point conversion = 2; 300–399 passing yards = +2.5; 400+ = +5; interception returned for TD = -2.

Receiving

Every receiving yard = 0.1; reception = 1; receiving TD = 6; 2-point receiving conversion = 2; 100–199 receiving yards = +2.5; 200+ = +5.

Rushing

Every rushing yard = 0.1; rushing TD = 6; 2-point rushing conversion = 2; 100–199 rushing yards = +2.5; 200+ = +5.

Kicking

PAT made = 1; PAT missed = -1; FG missed = -1; 30–39 = 3; 40–49 = 4; 50–59 = 5; 60+ = 6.

Miscellaneous

Kickoff return TD = 6; punt return TD = 6; fumble recovered for TD = 6; interception return TD = 6; fumble returned for TD = 6; blocked punt/FG return TD = 6; fumble lost = -2; 2-point return = 2; 1-point safety = 1.

2026 Draft

Draft Central

Draft Order

Filippo → Nicky → Tone → Frankie → Carmelo → Martir → Melio → Mark → Antonio → Berger → Skin → Grasso

Division Draft Order

Berger → Antonio → Mark → Melio → Melio → Mark → Antonio → Berger

Draft Pick Format

Picks 1–6 are a lottery. Worst record gets 1 vote; best non-playoff team gets 6. Picks 7–8 go to first-round playoff losers; 9–10 to second-round playoff losers; #11 to runner-up; #12 to champion. No draft-pick trading.

Keeper Rule

One keeper in Round 3 or later, using the round in which they were drafted. The player must remain on the team all year. Keeper lock is August 24 at noon.
